Local Gmail CLI / Privacy

Effective September 14, 2026

Privacy policy

This policy covers Local Gmail CLI, a personal email utility operated by Erafat Rehim. It describes the tool and its owner-directed inbox-cleaning workflow. It does not describe the privacy practices of podcast platforms or other services linked from the AED website.

Data accessed and purpose

With Google authorization, the tool accesses account identity, message identifiers, senders and recipients, subjects, message text, labels, and message counts. The accompanying cleanup workflow may retrieve selected attachments or receipts using authorized mail tools. This information supports email search, inbox organization, receipt filing, summaries, archiving, and owner-requested draft preparation.

Processing and sharing

The command-line utility runs on the owner's computer and communicates with Google's APIs. When the owner uses an AI assistant such as Codex to review messages, selected email content and tool results are also processed by that assistant's service under the owner's account settings and the provider's applicable terms. This workflow is therefore not exclusively local processing.

The tool does not sell email data or use it for advertising. Email contents, receipts, and authorization tokens are not published on this website. This public information page contains no Gmail sign-in form and does not request access to visitors' mailboxes.

Storage and retention

Google authorization tokens are stored locally for subsequent access and automatic renewal. They are kept out of public website files and source-control repositories. Selected receipts, summaries, and cleanup logs can be retained in the owner's personal files, including cloud-synchronized storage and assistant task history. Their retention is controlled by the owner and the relevant storage or assistant service; the cleanup workflow does not impose an automatic deletion schedule.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke the tool's connection through Google Account connections and remove its locally saved authorization. Revoking access stops future authorized requests but does not erase previously saved receipts, summaries, backups, or assistant history. Those copies must be removed through their respective storage or service controls.

Website hosting

These information pages are hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process ordinary web-request information such as IP addresses and request logs under its own privacy statement. These pages do not include analytics scripts, advertising, or an email-content upload form.

Contact and changes

For questions about this tool or retained data, contact alpha.288@gmail.com. Updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised effective date.
