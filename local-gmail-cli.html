Personal email organization

Local Gmail CLI

A personal utility operated by Erafat Rehim to search, read, count, and organize his own Gmail messages. This page is hosted on his Audio Epilepsy Digest website to explain the tool shown on Google's authorization screen.

What it does

The tool supports inbox review, receipt filing, message archiving, and draft preparation. It can be used through a local command-line interface or an owner-directed AI assistant such as Codex.

Google access

The tool requests Gmail permissions to read and modify messages and manage drafts. These permissions are broader than the daily cleanup workflow: the cleaner searches and reads mail, saves selected receipts and summaries, and archives messages under the owner's instructions. Its standing instructions prohibit sending, forwarding, deleting mail, and unsubscribing.

Authorization is granted through Google. Saved access can be renewed automatically until it expires or is revoked. The owner can remove access in Google Account connections.

Who uses it

This is a personal-use utility, not a public email service. Visiting the AED website or listening to the podcast does not connect a Gmail account.
